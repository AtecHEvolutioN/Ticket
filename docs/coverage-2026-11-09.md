## Fixes from the real pages

- Czech, Spanish, and Dutch best-available display controls; Dutch cookie rejection; bounded wait for delayed controls.
- Exact localized per-ticket text: Czech `za kus`, Dutch `per stuk`, German `je`, Spanish `cada una`, Swedish `per biljett`, and English `each`.
- Czech and Dutch resale labels and listing counts; duplicate cards no longer risk being counted twice.
- Swedish currency resolved through unambiguous matching-event metadata, without importing metadata prices or availability.
- UK quick-pick prices retain section/row context; purchase/search quantities and result counts remain separate from stock.

## Method and limits

Used ordinary graphical Chromium and verified artist-to-event links. Some pages needed longer rendering waits; one opened a new tab, and one US page required dismissing an ordinary informational popup. Only display controls and optional-cookie rejection were used. No tickets were selected or reserved, no presale codes entered, and no messages sent.

The background worker currently navigates directly to event URLs, so successful interactive navigation does not establish unattended access. Fresh availability can change immediately. Primary categories/ranges do not establish the quantity that can be fulfilled, and partial listing rows cannot establish all remaining stock.
