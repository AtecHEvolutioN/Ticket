Price observations

Candles summarize the first, highest, lowest and last minimum asking price recorded in each time bucket. They are not trades. Category price ranges do not prove ticket availability.

02

Counts, not sales

Bars use the last recorded count in a bucket. Polls are never added together. Reported quantity sums explicit amounts across distinct entries, which may overlap; it is not total event stock.

03

Gaps stay gaps

Missing or blocked price readings stay gaps. A displayed price can persist while stock is unknown. Sources, currencies, price basis and fees stay separate. This is an exported snapshot, not a live feed.
